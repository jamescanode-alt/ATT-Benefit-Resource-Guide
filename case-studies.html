How this works

Work each case like a first meeting

Read the client facts

Each case opens with a profile. Everything you need is in the facts, or in the field guide if you want to look it up. Using the guide is encouraged; that is the workflow on a real client.

Calculate or choose

Numeric questions want a number (rounding is fine; $ and % signs are OK). Multiple choice locks out wrong picks. A hint appears after a miss, and you can reveal the answer after two misses.

Advance and review

A correct answer shows the full reasoning with a link back to the guide section. Finish a case to unlock the next. First-attempt answers count toward your score.

Figures follow the illustrative values used in the Union / Bargained field guide (representative pension bands, factors, and thresholds). Real client numbers always come from NetBenefits and the controlling SPD.

Factors, bands, thresholds, and dollar limits vary by collective bargaining agreement and plan year and change over time. Nothing here is legal, tax, or investment advice or a promise of any benefit.
