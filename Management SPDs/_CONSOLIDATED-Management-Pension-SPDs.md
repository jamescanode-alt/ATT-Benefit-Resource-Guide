**The 2015 cliff is the single most consequential date in the management landscape**, and nothing in
the client's benefits portal announces the absence of a plan. A manager hired Dec. 31, 2014 accrues a
cash-balance pension; one hired Jan. 1, 2015 does not, and never will. This is also why the **enhanced
133⅓% / 100% 401(k) match** applies to exactly the group hired on or after Jan. 1, 2015 — the richer
match is the *replacement* for the pension, not a bonus on top of it (see [Part 5](#53-company-match-for-management)).

## Part 3 — Program specifics

### 3A — Nonbargained Program (NIN 78-49754, eff. Jan 1, 2020)

**Eligibility.** You are an Eligible Employee if you are a **Management Employee** of a
Participating Company (Appendix A) **and** were employed by a Participating Company on
**Dec 31, 2006** and were **not** rehired after that date. (Plain English: certain legacy SBC
management hired/rehired before Jan 1, 2007.) Hired/rehired after Dec 31, 2006 → not eligible
here (see Management Cash Balance Program).

*Not eligible* if: excluded by a special rule; already accruing under another program; a leased
employee or independent contractor; a nonresident alien with no U.S.-earned income; a Puerto Rico
resident; on a temporary/acting management promotion of ≤1 year; a Bargaining Unit or
Nonmanagement-Nonunion employee; or currently an Eligible Employee under the AT&T Legacy
Management Program, AT&T Puerto Rico Pension Benefit Plan, Mobility Program, or Southeast
Management Program. Special transfer/promotion rules exist (e.g., transfers before Dec 1, 2008).

**Participation & vesting.** Become a Participant after **1 Year of Participation Service**
(a 12-month Period of Service). **Vested** at **3 Years of Vesting Service**, or upon reaching
Normal Retirement Age while an employee. (Different rules if termination was before Jan 1, 2010.)

**Benefit = the greatest of three formulas:**

1. **CAM (Updated Career Average Minimum) Formula** — `1.6% × CAM Income ÷ 12`.
   - **CAM Income** = (average annual Pension Compensation for the 5-year period ending
     Dec 31, 1999 × Pension Calculation Service as of Dec 31, 1999) **+** Pension Compensation
     after Dec 31, 1999.
   - **Pension Compensation** = monthly wages incl. base pay; group incentive (annual bonus/Team
     Award); individual nondiscretionary incentive (commissions); group incentive adjustments
     (≥2000); Ameritech special contribution awards (≥2001); lump-sum special payments (≥2004);
     officer Incentive Plan payments (promoted ≥2009); STD payments while an employee.
2. **Cash Balance Formula** — for certain legacy **SBC, PTG, SNET** participants, plus Ameritech
   managers hired/rehired/promoted on/after Jan 1, 2001. **Basic benefit credits frozen
   Jan 14, 2005**; interest credits continue (monthly equivalent of the 30-Year U.S. Treasury
   rate from the middle month of the prior quarter).
3. **PBM (Pension Band Minimum) Formula** — for a **PBM Eligible Participant** (promoted into this
   Program from a **Banded Program** — Midwest, Southwest, or West — on/after June 13, 2001, with
   an accrued benefit in that program). Greater of:
   (a) `Pension Calculation Service × pre-promotion pension-band amount` + any Supplemental
   Monthly Pension Benefit accrued at promotion; or
   (b) the monthly benefit earned under the prior program at promotion **+** `1.2% × Pension
   Compensation paid in this Program ÷ 12`. No double-counting of service.

**Pension Calculation Service** = Term of Employment, but only continuous employment with a
Participating Company, and excluding any service used for a benefit under another plan/program.
Special rules: promotion from bargained positions; MPA-recognized service; service under another
AT&T plan (offset); **part-time prorated** by scheduled hours ÷ full-time schedule.

**Normal Retirement Age** = 65th birthday, or if later the 5th anniversary of plan participation.

**Early Retirement Pension (CAM/PBM only — *not* Cash Balance).** Turns on the **Modified Rule of 75**
(met on/before Termination of Employment):

| Age | Term of Employment |
|---|---|
| Any age | 30 years |
| 50 | 25 years |
| 55 | 20 years |
| 65+ | 10 years |

- **If you satisfy Mod 75:** no reduction if your Benefit Commencement Date is on/after age 55.
  Before age 55, reduce **0.5%/month (6%/yr)**; but with **≥30 years** Term of Employment the
  reduction is **0.25%/month (3%/yr)**.
- **If you do not satisfy Mod 75:** multiply the age-65 benefit by the **early retirement factor**
  for your age at BCD ([Appendix B factors](#appendix-b--nonbargained-early-retirement-factors)).

**Forms of payment.** Default: **Single Life Annuity** (no spouse) or **50% J&S** (spouse).
Alternates require spousal consent (notarized). Annuity amounts as % of SLA: **50% J&S = 90%**,
**75% J&S = 85%**, **100% J&S = 80%**. **Pop-up** (benefit restored to SLA level if the survivor
predeceases you) applies **only if Mod 75 was met**. J&S may be waived via QDRO.

**Lump sum.**
- **Full lump sum** available if you **terminated on/after May 25, 2018** and your **BCD is on/after
  June 1, 2018** (entire benefit; no monthly pension).
- If terminated **before** May 25, 2018: lump sum only if your benefit is in whole or part a Cash
  Balance (or other LS-eligible) formula. CAM/PBM lump sum = Actuarial Equivalent of the SLA.
- **CAM Excess Calculation** (when CAM/PBM also have a Cash Balance/other LS formula):
  1. Convert CAM/PBM benefit to a lump sum.
  2. Take the Cash Balance account (or LS value of the other formula).
  3. Subtract (2) from (1).
  4. Convert the difference back to an SLA.
  - Difference **< $400/mo** → may take **entire** benefit as a lump sum.
  - Difference **≥ $400/mo** → **partial** lump sum (= the cash-balance amount) plus a residual
    monthly annuity (SLA, 50% J&S, or 75% J&S) for the remainder.
- Repayment of a prior lump sum is allowed after reinstatement via settlement/award/order (with interest).

**Survivor benefits (death before BCD, vested).**
- **CAM/PBM:** spouse/Legally Recognized Partner may elect an SLA = **50%** of your would-be SLA.
  *Special rule* (active with ≥15 yrs Term of Employment, or active satisfying Mod 75, or a
  terminated participant who satisfied Mod 75): a 50% J&S-based benefit, **not reduced** for early
  commencement (not deferrable).
- **Cash Balance account:** spouse may elect an SLA derived from the account (in lieu of, not in
  addition to, the CAM/PBM survivor annuity); or a single lump sum of the account.
- **Lump sum** survivor option for CAM/PBM if you terminated on/after May 25, 2018 and hadn't
  commenced before Jun 1, 2018 (Actuarial Equivalent, using the survivor's age).
- Deferred commencement options exist for both CAM/PBM and Cash Balance survivors.
- No spouse/beneficiary → cash-balance lump sum paid per the beneficiary hierarchy.

**Retiree Death Benefit** (Nonbargained only). Payable to an Eligible Beneficiary if you meet
company-specific date tests (Ameritech, Pacific Telesis Group, SNET, Southwestern Bell — e.g.,
began a service/disability pension on/before April 30, 1995 and not rehired on/after Jan 1, 1993;
other companies have their own dates). **Amount** is based on **Wages** (base pay for full-time
service as of a company-specific date); max = greater of (1× Wages reduced **10% per full year**
since termination) **or** (monthly Pension Benefit × 12). **Eligible beneficiaries** in order:
spouse → Legally Recognized Partner → unmarried dependent children < 23 → dependent parents. Paid
as a **lump sum**. **Claims within 1 year of death.** (The beneficiary-hierarchy/Designation rules
do **not** govern this benefit.)

**Special-provision groups (Attachment 1):** Ameritech Defined Lump Sum (DLS) Formula & DLS
Account; Ameritech Special Pension Account (SPA); Ameritech Transition Formula; APVI Employee;
AT&T Mobility Employee (Cingular transfer service credit); PTG Accelerated Transition Benefit (ATB)
& Special ATB; Retired IBM Transferees; SBC Pre-Cash Balance Participants; SBC Special Pension
Account (SPA); SNET Management Pension Plan Participants. Contact the Recordkeeper to confirm.

### 3B — Mobility Program (NIN 78-49752, eff. Jan 1, 2020)

**Eligibility.** You are an Eligible Employee of a Participating Company (Appendix A) if you are
either: a **Management Employee** employed by a Participating Company on **Dec 31, 2005** and not
rehired on/after Jan 1, 2006; **or** a **Bargaining Unit Employee** covered by an Applicable
Collective Bargaining Agreement listed in Appendix A. Management hired/rehired after Dec 31, 2005 →
not eligible (see Management Cash Balance Program).

*Not eligible* (bargained exclusions include): covered by the **Orange Contract** (CWA Districts 1,
2-13, 4, 7, 9) and hired/rehired on/after **Jan 1, 2010** or transferred into it on/after Apr 6, 2013;
hired/rehired/transferred into the **Black Contract** (CWA District 3) on/after **Jan 1, 2011**;
various bargained transfer-ins (e.g., AT&T Southwest Core ≥ Apr 4, 2012; several units ≥ Aug 17, 2012;
BellSouth/AT&T Southeast units ≥ Sep 28 / Dec 7, 2012). Also excluded: leased employees, independent
contractors, nonresident aliens with no U.S. income, Puerto Rico residents, and anyone currently
eligible under the AT&T Legacy Management, AT&T Puerto Rico, Nonbargained, or Southeast Management
programs. *Special rule:* AT&T Wireless Services (AWS) employees active on Dec 31, 2005 became
eligible Jan 1, 2006.

**Participation & vesting.** Same as Nonbargained: Participant after 1 Year of Participation
Service; **vested at 3 Years of Vesting Service** (or NRA while employed).

**Benefit = Cash Balance Account only** (`How Your Cash Balance Account Is Calculated`).
- Hypothetical account starting at **$0** at participation (a transfer-in opens at $0 unless you
  already have a Cash Balance Account here).
- **Basic Benefit Credits** = **5% of Pension Compensation**, credited monthly (last day of month,
  or termination/transfer date if earlier).
  - **Pension Compensation** here = W-2 wages + before-tax elective deferrals under IRC §§125, 457,
    132(f) + before-tax salary deferrals to a nonqualified deferred-comp plan. *(Note: a different,
    broader definition than the Nonbargained CAM definition.)*
- **Interest Credits** = applicable monthly rate (compounding to the annual 30-Year U.S. Treasury
  rate published as of the **second month of the prior calendar quarter**) × prior month-end balance.
  Continue accruing after termination until the month before payments begin.
- *New participants:* credits begin the later of the date you became an Eligible Employee or
  12 months before first eligibility.

**Normal Retirement Age = 65** (no 5th-anniversary extension).

**No Modified Rule of 75 / no early-retirement reduction** — the early-payment benefit simply *is*
your Cash Balance Account (converted to an annuity by age factor if you elect an annuity).

**Forms of payment** (three annuities — **no 100% J&S**):
- **SLA:** Cash Balance Account ÷ an age-based annuity factor using the **1994 Group Annuity Reserve
  mortality table** and the 30-Year Treasury rate (middle month of prior quarter). *Example:*
  $100,000 at age 65 ÷ factor 141.5292 ≈ **$706.57/mo**.
- **50% J&S:** if you **first became a Participant on/after Jan 1, 2006** → **Actuarial Equivalent**
  (both ages); if you were a **Participant as of Dec 31, 2005** → **greater of 90% of SLA or the
  Actuarial Equivalent**. **Pop-up applies** (always). **Cannot** be waived via QDRO.
- **75% J&S:** Actuarial Equivalent. Pop-up applies. Cannot be waived via QDRO.
- **Lump sum:** always available = your **Cash Balance Account** on the BCD.

**Survivor benefits (death before BCD, vested).**
- **Annuity:** spouse may elect an SLA = the **greater of** (a) 50% of the benefit payable as a
  50% J&S the day before death, or (b) the SLA that is the Actuarial Equivalent of the Cash Balance
  Account (survivor's age).
- **Single lump sum** = the Cash Balance Account (spouse or Designated Beneficiary).
- No spouse / Legally Recognized Partner / Designated Beneficiary → lump sum to your **estate**.

**No Retiree Death Benefit** under the Mobility Program.

**Special-provision groups (Attachment 1):** AT&T Mobility Employee (Cingular transfer credit);
Contributed Management Employees (SBC/BellSouth → Cingular); Employees Affected by Certain Corporate
Transactions; Former PTG Bargained Employees (frozen **District 9 Program**); Former SNET Bargained
Employees (frozen **District 1 Program**).

## Part 4 — Common provisions (apply to both 2020 programs)

These sections are materially identical across the Nonbargained and Mobility 2020 SPDs (both
programs of the AT&T Component Part). Program-specific exceptions are flagged inline.

**Break in Service rules.**
- *Vesting:* a break of **≥5 years before** you are vested wipes out prior Years of Vesting Service;
  a break **<5 years** is handled under bridging rules. Once **vested**, breaks don't undo it.
- *Term of Employment:* an absence **≤6 months** counts (prior service included immediately on
  rehire, but the absence period itself is excluded); an absence **>6 months** → Term of Employment
  counts only from rehire, **unless** you complete **5 years** of continuous service after rehire,
  which restores the prior period. *Layoff/involuntary reduction:* rehire within **2 years** is not
  a break (absence ≤6 months in a 12-month period is included).
- Military Leave of Absence and litigation/arbitration/grievance reinstatements have special rules.
- You stop earning service for all purposes at Termination of Employment.

**Effect of rehire within the AT&T Controlled Group.** After a prior lump sum or annuity, a rehire
into a participating program reduces your post-rehire benefit by what you already received. If you
are receiving a monthly annuity and are rehired into a **management** position, the annuity is **not**
suspended; rehire into any **other** position **suspends** it. The prior annuity's payment form
doesn't change.

**Moving between members of the AT&T Controlled Group.** Your benefit = the sum of two pieces. After
**3 years** of service in the new program, prior-program service is included in Pension Calculation
Service (unless earned under a cash-balance formula), but your benefit is **offset** by the
prior-program benefit. You can receive distributions from each applicable program at termination.

**Claims & appeals.**
- No claim is needed just to start your pension — but you must **elect** to begin before NRA or to
  choose an alternate form.
- **Initial claim denial:** written notice generally within **90 days** (one **90-day** extension
  possible), stating reasons, plan provisions, and appeal rights.
- **Appeal:** file in writing within **60 days** of the denial, to the Benefit Plan Committee.
- **Appeal decision:** generally within **60 days** of receipt (one **60-day** extension possible).
- You must **exhaust** these procedures before suing.
- **Time to file suit:** **Nonbargained** — within **1 year** after the appeal is denied.
  **Mobility** — within **90 days** of the final determination; or, for eligibility/benefit denials,
  **2 years** from (a) a lump-sum payment, (b) a first periodic payment, or (c) the date of the act
  complained of.

**Mandatory cash-out (as amended).** Effective **Jan. 1, 2024** (plan-wide SMM NIN 78-72720, July
2024), the small-benefit threshold rose from **$5,000 to $7,000** and now works in two tiers: if the
present value is **$1,000 or less** it is paid as a single lump sum as soon as administratively
practicable (a direct-rollover election may be made); if it is **more than $1,000 but not more than
$7,000** it is paid as an **automatic direct rollover to an IRA** unless the participant elects a
single lump sum or a different rollover. Distributions to a surviving spouse, other beneficiary, or
alternate payee are paid as a single lump sum. The same $7,000 figure is referenced in the
**Restrictions Based on Funding Level** provisions.

**Retiree Death Benefit cap.** Effective per SMM **NIN 78-63911** (July 2022), a **$25,000 cap**
applies to the death benefit payable to a surviving beneficiary of eligible retirees who terminated
**on or after Jan. 1, 1992**. This applies across the AT&T Programs of the AT&T Component Part and the
AT&T Retiree Death Benefit Program under AT&T Umbrella Benefit Plans No. 1 and No. 3. Where the
Nonbargained material in Part 3A describes an uncapped Retiree Death Benefit, read it subject to this
cap.

**Administration.** The **Plan Administrator** determines eligibility, benefit rights/amounts, and
interprets the Plan (full discretion). Appeals/interpretation are delegated to the **Benefit Plan
Committee**; investing the Pension Fund is delegated to the **Benefit Plan Investment Committee**.

**Amendment or termination.** The **Senior EVP–Human Resources of AT&T Inc.** may amend the Plan at
any time. No amendment/termination can reduce a benefit already accrued (except as law allows). On
full/partial termination you become vested.

**PBGC.** Generally covers normal/early retirement benefits, certain pre-termination disability
benefits, and certain survivor benefits, up to statutory maximums; does **not** cover amounts above
the maximum, certain benefit increases in place < 5 years, non-vested benefits, certain
Social-Security-supplement early-retirement amounts, or non-pension benefits. PBGC:
Office of Benefits Administration, P.O. Box 151750, Alexandria, VA 22315; 800-400-7242; pbgc.gov.

**General plan information.**
- **Missing participants / addresses:** keep current addresses on file; unclaimed benefits stay in
  the fund and may be forfeited after legal requirements (reinstated if a valid claim is later made).
- **Top-heavy rules:** automatic minimum benefit / accelerated vesting if the Plan ever became
  top-heavy (unlikely).
- **No assignment / QDRO:** benefits can't be assigned or attached, **except** under a **Qualified
  Domestic Relations Order** paying an alternate payee (spouse, former spouse, child, dependent).
- **No double crediting:** offsets prevent counting the same service/compensation twice across programs.
- **Direct rollover:** a lump-sum **Eligible Rollover Distribution** can roll to a 401(a)/IRA/403(a)/
  403(b)/governmental-457(b) plan; otherwise **20%** mandatory federal withholding applies.
- **Designation of beneficiaries:** designate via Fidelity's Online Beneficiary tool (spousal
  consent required to name someone other than a spouse). If none on file, the **Beneficiary

## Part 5 — The AT&T Retirement Savings Plan (ARSP), the management 401(k)

> **Scope note.** Parts 1–4 cover the **pension**. This Part is a deliberate extension, added because
> the **ARSP SPD (NIN 78-74968, July 2026)** is now in this library and because, for management hired
> on or after Jan. 1, 2015, the ARSP is the *only* AT&T retirement plan they have (see the note on the
> Management Cash Balance Program's closure in Part 2). Source: ARSP SPD/Prospectus, July 2026.

### 5.1 Plan identifiers

Management employees are Eligible Employees with **immediate** plan eligibility. A management
employee is one whose job title and classification are **not** covered by a collective bargaining
agreement; the definition also sweeps in nonmanagement nonunion employees except those following
certain bargaining units. **Not eligible:** anyone eligible to contribute to another AT&T savings or
profit-sharing plan (the BellSouth Savings and Security Plan, or the former AT&T Savings and Security
Plan); leased employees and independent contractors; employees of a service provider; non-U.S.
residents with no U.S.-sourced income; and **AT&T Enterprises, LLC management employees classified as
"on-call"** (formerly AT&T Support Services, Inc.).

### 5.2 Enrollment and employee contributions

- **Automatic enrollment** at a **3% before-tax Basic Contribution**, beginning with the first full
  payroll period **60 days** after becoming eligible, escalating **1% per year up to 6%**. Any
  affirmative change to the contribution percentage **stops the escalation immediately and
  permanently** — worth checking for a client who once adjusted their rate and has been flat since.
- **Default investment (QDIA):** the **AT&T Age-Based Asset Allocation Fund** matching the
  participant's age, assuming retirement at 65.
- **Basic Contributions** up to **50% of Compensation** in 1% increments, as before-tax, after-tax,
  Roth, or any combination. Only the **first 6%** is ever matched.
- **Voluntary Annual Increase Program:** whole percentages up to **10%** per year, until Basic
  Contributions reach 50%.
- **Spillover** is the default: on hitting the statutory deferral limit, contributions automatically
  continue as **After-tax Contributions**, which keeps the per-pay-period match alive. Turning
  spillover off stops contributions at the limit — and stops the match with them.
- **Catch-up** requires a **separate election** in 1% increments and is rejected unless the Basic
  Contribution election is above 0%. It is **never matched**, is **exempt from 415(c)**, and is
  **excluded from spillover** (it simply stops at the limit).

### 5.3 Company Match for management

| Group | Match | Max % of pay |
|---|---|---|
| Management Employees (general) | **80%** of the first 6% of Compensation | 4.8% |
| AT&T Management **and** NMNU following CWA (AT&T Core), hired/rehired/transferred **on or after Jan. 1, 2015** | **133⅓%** of the first 3% **+ 100%** of the next 3% | **7.0%** |
| Former DIRECTV, DIRECTV hire date **on or after Jan. 1, 2016**, transferred to AT&T Management with no intervening termination | 133⅓% / 100% | 7.0% |
| Former DIRECTV, DIRECTV hire date **before Jan. 1, 2016**, transferred on/after July 24, 2015 | 80% of the first 6% | 4.8% |

Management employees are eligible for the match **upon hire** — no service wait, unlike the one Year
of Service most bargained groups serve. Under the tiered formula the match is **4% of Compensation at
a 3% contribution** and **7% at 6%**, and it never exceeds 7%.

> **The 2015 date is not a coincidence.** The Management Cash Balance Program closed to hires and
> rehires after **Dec. 31, 2014**, and the enhanced 133⅓%/100% match applies to management hired on or
> after **Jan. 1, 2015**. The richer match is the **replacement** for the pension, not a supplement to
> it. Two managers with adjacent hire dates and identical titles have structurally different
> retirement plans, and for the post-2015 manager the 401(k) is the entire plan.

**Where the match lands.** For most management groups the Company Match is invested in the **AT&T
Shares Fund** on the same day the Basic Contributions post, building single-stock exposure to the
employer by default. Exceptions whose match follows the employee's own elections: **Technical
Services** employees, **AT&T National Contract IBEW** (hired on or before Aug. 8, 2009),
**Cricket/AIO Wireless, LLC**, and **AT&T Investment Operations I, LLC**. Diversification is
**immediate and unlimited** — any participant may exchange or transfer up to 100% of the match,
vested or not.

**Vesting.** Employee contributions are always 100% vested. The Company Match vests **100% after 3
Years of Service**, and immediately on **death, Disability, or age 65**. Unvested match is forfeited
at Termination of Employment, held for five years or until the account is fully distributed. It can
be **restored** on rehire within five years if the participant repays the distribution (from a
qualified plan or IRA — cash that was not rolled over is nonqualified and cannot be restored).

### 5.4 2026 limits

| Limit | 2026 figure |
|---|---|
| 402(g) elective deferrals (before-tax + Roth combined) | **$24,500** |
| 415(c) total annual additions | **$72,000** |
| Catch-up, age 50+ | **$8,000** |
| Catch-up, ages 60–63 | **$11,250** |
| Highly compensated employee threshold (2025 Compensation) | **$160,000** |
| Annual compensation limit | **$360,000** |
| Mandatory cash-out ceiling | **$7,000** |

Two operational points sit behind these. On reaching the **$360,000 compensation limit, contributions
are automatically suspended for the remainder of the year** — which for a senior manager on a level
contribution percentage can silently stop the per-pay-period match before December. And **highly
compensated employees** (2025 Compensation above $160,000, which covers much of this population) can
have before-tax, after-tax, or match contributions **reduced, recharacterized, or partially returned**
through nondiscrimination testing, so an aggressive after-tax target is a target, not a guarantee.

### 5.5 Roth catch-up is now mandatory for higher earners

The July 2026 SPD states the SECURE 2.0 rule as **currently in force**: a participant who is age 50 or
older by the end of the calendar year and earned **more than $150,000 in FICA wages in 2025** must
make catch-up contributions **on a Roth basis only**. For a management population this is the common
case rather than the exception, and for anyone who has been making **pre-tax** catch-up contributions
it is a real current-year tax increase that should be anticipated rather than discovered on a
paycheck.

Participants who turn **60, 61, 62, or 63** during the year are defaulted toward the higher **$11,250**
limit and must log in to NetBenefits to *reduce* it.

### 5.6 Investments, loans, withdrawals, distributions

- **Fidelity BrokerageLink:** minimum **$1,000** to open, **$500** on subsequent contributions,
  capped at **90%** of payroll contributions and of the total vested balance. Individual **AT&T**
  stock and AT&T corporate bonds are excluded from what may be bought inside it. The Plan may
  restrict direct trading from the **AT&T Stable Value Fund** into BrokerageLink, which it may deem
  a "competing" option. BrokerageLink and Dividend Fund Account balances are **not available for
  loans**.
- **Loans:** up to **three outstanding**, of which **one** may be a principal-residence loan (1–10
  years); general purpose runs 1–5 years. Minimum **$1,000**; maximum the lesser of **$50,000 less
  the highest outstanding balance in the prior 12 months** or **50% of the vested balance**, counted
  **across all AT&T Controlled Group plans**. Interest is **prime + 1%**. Executive officers subject
  to Section 16 of the Exchange Act **may not** take a loan. Default occurs if a missed payment is not
  cured by the end of the following calendar quarter; the balance is then reported as taxable income,
  and the default clears only on repayment or at age 59½.
- **Withdrawals while employed:** General, Before-tax Hardship, SOP, Roth, Rollover, Military, and
  **Domestic Abuse**. Before-tax money is reachable through a General Withdrawal only if **Disabled or
  age 59½**; after-tax money and vested match are available at any age. The **Domestic Abuse
  Withdrawal** is the lesser of **$10,500 or 50%** of the vested account, taken within one year of the
  incident and repayable within three years.
- **Distributions:** full lump sum; up to **eight partial distributions per year**; monthly,
  quarterly, or annual installments (revocable at any time, with no forced payout of the remainder).
  **Mandatory cash-out:** **≤ $1,000** paid as a lump sum; **more than $1,000 but not more than
  $7,000** automatically **direct-rolled to an IRA** unless the participant elects otherwise; above
  **$7,000** nothing happens without an election. A distribution **cannot be paid for 15 days after an
  address change**.
- **Roth In-Plan Rollover:** eligible before-tax and after-tax money may be converted to Roth within
  the plan. The election is **irrevocable** and cannot be undone even if the Roth account then falls
  in value. This is the second half of the mega-backdoor Roth, and its presence is what makes the
  strategy work in the ARSP.
- **RMDs:** the SPD's stated ages are 70½ (born on or before June 30, 1949), 72 (born July 1,
  1949 – Dec. 31, 1950), and **73** (born Jan. 1, 1951 – Dec. 31, 1958). The SPD **does not state an
  age for participants born in 1959 or later**, so confirm that group's applicable age separately
  rather than inferring it. **RMD rules do not apply to Roth Contributions.**

### 5.7 Related plan changes on file

- **Roth / non-Roth investing split**, effective **Sept. 21, 2026**: investment elections in the core
  lineup can be set separately for Roth and non-Roth money, and every BrokerageLink account is split
  into **BrokerageLink** (non-Roth) and **BrokerageLink Roth**. A blackout runs from approximately
  4 p.m. ET **Sept. 14, 2026** to approximately 9:30 a.m. ET **Sept. 22, 2026**. Third-party (advisor)

- **Mandatory cash-out** raised from **$1,000 to $7,000** (July 2026 SPD).
