# AT&T Union / Bargained Pension — Consolidated SPD & SMM Reference

**A single de-duplicated reference compiled from the AT&T Union/Bargained Summary Plan Descriptions (SPDs) and Summaries of Material Modifications (SMMs).**

This document states the language that is **common to all six bargained pension programs once**, then breaks out the **provisions that are unique to a specific program** and notes the **applicable union(s) / bargaining unit(s)** for each. It is a navigation and comparison aid only.

> **Governing-document notice (carried from every source SPD):** In all cases, the official Plan documents govern and are the final authority on the terms of the Plan. If there are any discrepancies between this reference (or any SPD/SMM) and the Plan, the Plan documents control. AT&T Inc. reserves the right to terminate or amend any of its employee benefit plans or programs at any time for any reason. Participation in the Plan is neither a contract nor a guarantee of future employment.

> **Plan-name note:** The 2020 SPDs were issued under the **AT&T/WarnerMedia Pension Benefit Plan** name (a temporary name used while WarnerMedia was part of AT&T). The July 2024 SMMs were issued under the restored name **AT&T Pension Benefit Plan**. They refer to the same Plan; the WarnerMedia reference is historical.

# Part 3 — Shared Provisions (common to all six programs)

The following provisions appear, in materially identical language, in all six bargained SPDs. They are stated once here. Where a program modifies one of these, the difference is called out in **Part 4**.

## 3.1 Plan structure and governing documents

Each SPD is a guide to the benefits under one **Program** of the AT&T Component Part of the AT&T/WarnerMedia (now AT&T) Pension Benefit Plan. The SPD, together with any subsequent SMMs issued for that Program, constitutes the SPD for that Program. The official Plan document controls in any conflict, and no rights accrue by reason of any statement shown in or omitted from an SPD. An SPD does not create a contract of employment with any member of the AT&T Controlled Group.

## 3.2 Eligible Employee (framework)

> "You are an Eligible Employee if you are an active Regular Employee or a Term Employee … [covered by] an Applicable Collective Bargaining Agreement listed in 'Appendix A' …, or your job title and classification are included in a collective bargaining agreement between a Participating Company and a [union]."

Eligibility, participation, benefit provisions, and forms of payment depend on factors including **employment status** (full-time/part-time), **job-title classification**, **employer**, and **service history** (hire date, Termination of Employment, and Term of Employment). The Recordkeeper can confirm these details for an individual.

## 3.3 Types of Pension Benefit

> "If you are a Participant, you are entitled to receive one (1) of two (2) types of Pension Benefits depending on your age and Term of Employment:
> - If your Termination of Employment date is on or after your Normal Retirement Age, you are entitled to receive a **Normal Retirement Pension**.
> - If your Termination of Employment date is before your Normal Retirement Age and you have a Vested Interest, you are entitled [to an **Early Retirement Pension** or deferred vested pension]."

A **Disability Pension** is available to participants who are Totally Disabled, have at least 15 years of service, and have **not** satisfied the Modified Rule of 75 (program variations in Part 4).

## 3.4 Pension-formula mechanics (general)

Two benefit formulas appear across the programs. A given program uses one, the other, a combination, or the greater of the two (see Part 2).

- **Pension Band Formula** — A monthly benefit equal to the **Pension Band amount multiplied by Pension Calculation Service**, plus a **Supplemental Monthly Pension Benefit**. The Pension Band amount is set by job-classification "band" tables negotiated in the applicable CBA.
  - *Illustrative calculation (from the Midwest SPD):* Band 216 (2016) = $66.51 × 28.75 = $1,912.16; Band 220 (2018) = $75.60 × 1.25 = $94.50; **Total monthly pension = $2,006.66.**
- **Cash Balance Formula** — A **hypothetical account** credited with **Basic Benefit / Pension Band Credits** plus **Interest Credits** (generally the 30-Year U.S. Treasury rate from the prior November, subject to a program minimum), and, where applicable, **Promotion Credits** and **Differential Pay Credits**. At distribution, the account may be paid as a lump sum or converted to an actuarially equivalent annuity.

## 3.5 Modified Rule of 75

The **Modified Rule of 75** is defined identically in all six bargained SPDs as a set of age and Term of Employment combinations (verbatim, from the East Program SPD):

> "Modified Rule of 75. You meet any of the following age and Term of Employment combinations at your Termination of Employment:"

| Age | Term of Employment |
|-----|--------------------|
| Any age | 30 years |
| 50 | 25 years |
| 55 | 20 years |
| 65 or older | 10 years |

> *Note: Employees who satisfy the Modified Rule of 75 (or are eligible for a Disability Pension) may also be eligible for certain retiree medical, dental, life and long term care insurance benefits. Refer to the relevant SPDs for more information about retiree coverage.*

**Important:** The Modified Rule of 75 is satisfied by meeting **at least one** of the defined bands above — it is **not** a simple "age plus service equals 75" test. For example, **any age with 30 years** of Term of Employment qualifies (e.g., age 40 + 30 years), while a combination that merely sums to 75 but does not reach a band (e.g., age 60 + 15 years) does **not** qualify. This identical table appears in the West, Southeast, Mobility, Midwest, East, and Legacy Bargained SPDs (and matches the corresponding non-bargained SPD definition).

Satisfying the Modified Rule of 75 generally provides a more favorable (less-reduced or unreduced) early pension and broader forms of payment; not satisfying it triggers actuarial reductions and, in some programs, minimum commencement ages. The **reduction percentages, reference ages, and commencement rules differ by program** — see Part 4 and the "Early Retirement Pension" section of the applicable Program SPD.

> **Do not confuse with East's Enhanced Annuity test.** The East Program separately conditions its Cash Balance **Enhanced Annuity** on a narrower table — *Any age / 30 years* or *Age 55 / 20 years* — which is distinct from the Modified Rule of 75 above (see §4.5).

## 3.6 Forms of payment (framework)

All programs offer monthly annuity options, generally including:

- **Single Life Annuity**
- **Joint and 50% / 75% / 100% Survivor Annuity** (with a "pop-up" feature in most programs if the Modified Rule of 75 is satisfied or for terminations after specified dates)
- **Lump Sum Distribution** (availability and election windows differ by program — see Part 2/Part 4)

A **Disability Pension** is generally paid as a Single Life Annuity (Midwest also offers J&S options).

## 3.7 Survivor benefits and other death benefits (framework)

A **Survivor Benefit** is available to a Spouse, Legally Recognized Partner, or other eligible/designated beneficiary (recipient categories vary slightly by program — see Part 2). Programs also provide one or more of: **Accident Death Benefit, Sickness Death Benefit, Retiree Death Benefit**, or (East) an **Active Employee Death Benefit**. **All claims for death benefits must be filed within one (1) year of death.**

## 3.8 Break-in-service, rehire, and movement rules

Each SPD contains common "Break in Service Rules" governing how a Year of Service is counted, how breaks affect Vested Interest and Term of Employment, the effect of rehire within the AT&T Controlled Group, and movement between members of the Controlled Group.

## 3.9 Claims and appeals procedures

> Standard across all six programs: A participant generally receives written notice of a denied claim within **ninety (90) days**; has **sixty (60) days** after receipt of a benefit denial to file a written appeal; generally receives a final determination within **sixty (60) days** of the appeal; must exhaust administrative remedies before filing suit; and is subject to the program's "Time to File Suit" limit (see Part 2). Separate **Disability Claims and Appeal Procedures** apply to Disability Pension claims.

## 3.10 Mandatory Cash-Out / Special Rule for Small Pension Benefits *(as amended — see Part 5)*

As amended by the **July 2024 SMM, effective January 1, 2024**, the following applies to **all** programs:

> - If the present value of your Pension Benefit is **$1,000 or less**, it is automatically paid in a single lump sum as soon as administratively practicable (a direct-rollover election may be made).
> - If the present value is **more than $1,000 but not more than $7,000**, it is automatically paid as a **direct rollover to an IRA** unless you elect a single lump-sum payment or a different rollover.
> - Distributions to a surviving spouse, other beneficiary, or alternate payee are paid in a single lump sum as soon as administratively practicable.

## 3.11 Funding restrictions, ERISA rights, and administrative information

Each SPD includes the standard **Restrictions Based on Funding Level** (as amended in 2024 — see Part 5), **Statement of ERISA Rights**, and **Administrative Information** (Plan sponsor, Plan Administrator, agent for service of process, Plan numbers, PBGC insurance, etc.).

# Part 4 — Program-Specific Provisions (unique language, by program/union)

Each subsection below captures only what is **unique to that program**. Anything not listed follows the shared provisions in Part 3.

## 4.1 West Program — *CWA District 9; IBEW Local 1269*

- **Applicable CBAs:** AT&T West Core Contract – CWA District 9; Pacific Bell Telephone Company – IBEW Local 1269.
- **Eligibility:** Bargained employees of the West Core CWA (**excluding Appendices D and E**) and West IBEW Local 1269 hired/rehired **before Aug. 9, 2009**; plus NMNUs hired/rehired on or before Aug. 8, 2009 who follow the AT&T West Core CWA agreement. Special rules apply for certain transfers, reclassifications, and surpluses.
- **Participation:** After 1 year of service. **Vesting:** 100% after **5 years** of service. **NRA:** later of age 65 / 5 years of participation.
- **Formula:** **Pension Band Formula** (Pension Band amount × Pension Calculation Service + Supplemental Monthly Pension Benefit).
- **Early Retirement Pension amount:**
  - 30 years of service → monthly annuity equal to the Pension Band Formula (unreduced);
  - Satisfies Modified Rule of 75 but < 30 years → Pension Band Formula **reduced by 0.5% for each month prior to age 55**;
  - Does not satisfy Modified Rule of 75 → reduced by Program factors.
- **Forms of payment:** Single Life Annuity; 50/75/100% J&S (with pop-up if Modified Rule of 75 satisfied); **lump sum if elected within 90 days of the termination date.**
- **Disability Pension eligibility:** Totally Disabled **and** 15 years of service **and** does not satisfy Modified Rule of 75.
- **Time to file suit:** No later than **five (5) years** from the date of final determination by the Benefit Plan Committee.

## 4.2 Southeast Program — *CWA District 3; AT&T Billing Southeast CWA; BellSouth ND/CA*

- **Applicable CBAs:** AT&T Southeast Core Contract – CWA District 3; AT&T Billing Southeast – CWA; BellSouth Telecommunications National Directory & Customer Assistance (ND/CA).
- **Eligibility:** Bargained employees of BellSouth Telecommunications (ND/CA); and bargained employees of the AT&T Southeast Core Contract CWA and AT&T Billing Southeast CWA hired/rehired **before Aug. 9, 2009.**
- **Participation:** **Immediate.**
- **Vesting:** 100% after **3 years** of service — **except Special Represented Employees**, who vest 100% after **5 years of participation** in the Plan.
- **Formula (unique two-track structure):**
  - Eligible Employees (excluding Special Represented) → **combination of the Cash Balance Formula and the Pension Band Formula.** The Pension Band component uses Pension Calculation Service **as of Dec. 31, 1998** and an **Age Incentive Factor.**
  - **Special Represented Employees** → **Pension Band Formula** (Pension Band amount × Pension Calculation Service × Age Incentive Factor + Supplemental Monthly Pension Benefit).
  - **Cash Balance Formula:** hypothetical account credited **annually** with Basic Benefit Credits (based on pension band) and Interest Credits (30-Year Treasury rate from prior November).
- **NRA:** later of age 65 / 5 years of service.
- **Early Retirement Pension amount:**
  - Cash Balance component → your Cash Balance Account;
  - Pension Band component → 30 years → unreduced; satisfies Modified Rule of 75 but < 30 years → **reduced by 0.5% for each month or partial month prior to age 56** (note: **age 56**, not 55); does not satisfy → Program factors.
  - Pension Band, if Modified Rule of 75 not satisfied: if ≥25 years of service may commence at age 50; if ≥20 years may commence at age 55.
- **Death benefits:** Other death benefits apply to a **"Death Benefit Eligible Participant."** Note a burial-benefit reference of up to **$500** and benefit amounts determined as of **Dec. 31, 2004** in certain provisions. **ND/CA employees** are treated separately for several survivor/death provisions.
- **Time to file suit:** Any suit based on a denial of eligibility and/or for benefits must be filed **no later than one hundred twenty (120) days** from the date of final determination by the Benefit Plan Committee (shorter than the 5-year limit used by West/Midwest/East/Legacy).

## 4.3 Mobility Bargained Program — *CWA District 6 (Purple Contract)*

- **Applicable CBA:** AT&T Mobility Services LLC – CWA District 6 (**Purple Contract**).
- **Eligibility:** AT&T Mobility CWA District 6 (Purple Contract) bargained employees hired/rehired **before Jan. 1, 2009** (note: earlier cutoff than the Aug. 9, 2009 used by most programs).
- **Participation:** After 1 year of service. **Vesting:** 100% after **5 years** of service. **NRA:** later of age 65 / 5 years of participation.
- **Formula:** **Pension Band Formula** (Pension Band amount × Pension Calculation Service + Supplemental Monthly Pension Benefit).
- **Early Retirement Pension — unique commencement rule when Modified Rule of 75 is NOT satisfied:**
  - If **at least 25 years of service**, the benefit may commence **on or after age 50**;
  - If **at least 20 years of service**, the benefit may commence **on or after age 55.**
- **Early Retirement Pension amount:** 30 years → unreduced; satisfies Modified Rule of 75 but < 30 years → reduced by 0.5% per month prior to age 55; otherwise Program factors.
- **Forms of payment:** Single Life Annuity; 50/75/100% J&S (pop-up if Modified Rule of 75 satisfied); lump sum if Modified Rule of 75 satisfied.
- **Time to file suit:** No later than the last day of the limitations period under the **applicable statute of limitations** (note: differs from the fixed 5-year limit used by West/Midwest/East/Legacy).

## 4.4 Midwest Program — *CWA District 4; IBEW System Council T-3 (Locals 21, 58, 134)*

- **Applicable CBAs:** AT&T Midwest Core Contract – CWA District 4 (incl. Appendix G / CPE); AT&T Midwest Contract – IBEW System Council T-3; IBEW Local 21 (Appendix D); IBEW Local 58 (Appendix E); IBEW Local 134 (Appendix F).
- **Eligibility:** Bargained employees of the IBEW and CWA **excluding** Appendix F of the Midwest Core CWA contract, Appendix C of the IBEW System Council T-3 Midwest Contract, and employees in the job title of **Sales Consultant** in the IBEW System Council T-3 Midwest Contract; hired/rehired **before Aug. 9, 2009.** Plus NMNUs hired/rehired on or before Aug. 8, 2009 who follow the AT&T Midwest Core CWA or IBEW System Council T-3 (Midwest) agreements.
- **Participation:** After 1 year of service **and at least age 21.** **Vesting:** 100% after **5 years** of service. **NRA:** later of age 65 / **4 years** of participation.
- **Formula:** **Pension Band Formula** (see the illustrative band calculation in §3.4).
- **Early Retirement Pension amount:** 30 years → unreduced; satisfies Modified Rule of 75 but < 30 years → reduced by 0.5% per month prior to age 55; otherwise Program factors.
- **Forms of payment:** Single Life Annuity; 50/75/100% J&S (pop-up if Modified Rule of 75 satisfied); lump sum. **Disability Pension** may be paid as Single Life Annuity **or** 50/75/100% J&S with pop-up (broader than most programs).
- **Survivor benefits:** Available for **Spouse or other Designated Beneficiary** (note: this program's at-a-glance does not list Legally Recognized Partner).
- **Time to file suit:** No later than **five (5) years** from the date of final determination by the Benefit Plan Committee.

## 4.5 East Program — *CWA District 1 / AT&T Corp. Core Contract CWA*

- **Applicable CBAs:** AT&T East Core Contract – CWA District 1 (**before Dec. 27, 2020**); AT&T Corp. Core Contract – CWA (**on and after Dec. 27, 2020**).
- **Eligibility:** Bargained employees of East Core CWA (**excluding Appendix F** of the East Core CWA contract) hired/rehired **before Aug. 9, 2009.** Special rules apply for transfers, reclassifications, surpluses, and **Converted East Employees.**
- **Participation:** After 1 year of service. **Vesting:** 100% after **3 years** of service. **NRA:** later of age 65 / 4 years of participation.
- **Formula — Cash Balance (unique among the Pension-Band programs):** hypothetical account credited **monthly** with:
  - **Basic Benefit Credits** — 1/12 of the applicable annual service credit based on Pension Compensation and Service Category;
  - **Interest Credits** — 30-Year U.S. Treasury rate from prior November, **minimum 4.00%** (note: higher floor than Legacy Bargained's 3.75%);
  - **Promotion Credits.**
- **Early Retirement Pension amount:** A monthly annuity equal to the **Cash Balance Account projected to Normal Retirement Age** then converted to an actuarially equivalent annuity; an **Enhanced Annuity** applies if (a) 30 years of service, or at least age 55 with 20 years of service at Termination of Employment, **and** (b) the benefit is elected as an annuity.
- **Forms of payment (broadest lump-sum options):** Single Life Annuity; 50/75% J&S (pop-up if terminated on/after Mar. 31, 1995; without pop-up if before); **Annuity with Lump Sum Certain** and **Partial Lump Sum** if elected within **180 days** of Termination of Employment (Temporary Employees excluded); full lump sum.
- **Death benefits:** **Active Employee Death Benefit** and **Retiree Death Benefit** (the only program of the six using the "Active Employee Death Benefit" label).
- **Time to file suit:** No later than **five years** from the date of final determination by the Benefit Plan Committee.

## 4.6 Legacy Bargained Program — *AT&T Corp. CWA; IBEW System Council T-3 (National Contract)*

- **Applicable CBAs:** AT&T Corp. Core Contract – CWA; AT&T Corp. National Contract – IBEW System Council T-3.
- **Eligibility:** Legacy AT&T Corp. CWA and IBEW System Council T-3 AT&T Corp. National Contract bargained employees hired/rehired **before Aug. 9, 2009**; plus NMNU employees hired/rehired before Aug. 9, 2009 who follow the Legacy AT&T Corp. CWA bargaining agreement.
- **Participation:** **Immediate, if at least age 21.** **Vesting:** 100% after **3 years** of service. **NRA:** later of age 65 / **3 years** of service (lowest service threshold of the six).
- **Formula — Greater of two formulas:** the participant receives the **greater of the Cash Balance Formula and the Pension Band Formula.**
  - **Cash Balance Formula:** hypothetical account credited **annually** with **Pension Band Credits**, **Interest Credits** (30-Year U.S. Treasury rate from prior November, **minimum 3.75%**), **Differential Pay Credits**, and **Promotion Credits.**
  - **Pension Band Formula:** for certain employees with **Term of Employment > 15 years as of June 30, 1998** — Pension Band amount × Pension Calculation Service + Supplemental Monthly Pension Benefit.
- **Early Retirement Pension amount:** Cash Balance → a monthly annuity equal to the Cash Balance Account divided by Program factors; Pension Band → 30 years → unreduced, else reduced by 0.5% per month prior to age 55.
- **Forms of payment:** Single Life Annuity; 50/75% J&S w/ pop-up; **100% J&S w/ pop-up** and **Single Life Annuity with 10-Year Certain** if terminated on/after **July 1, 1999**; **lump sum only if the benefit is paid under the Cash Balance Formula.**
- **Survivor benefits:** Available for **Spouse, Legally Recognized Partner, or estate.**
- **Death benefit:** **Retiree Death Benefit** available to certain participants with Terminations of Employment **prior to Jan. 1, 2008.**
- **Program SMM:** A program-specific July 2024 SMM also applies (identical $7,000 cash-out change — see Part 5).
- **Time to file suit:** No later than **five (5) years** from the date of final determination by the Benefit Plan Committee.

# Part 5 — Plan-Wide Modifications (2024 SMMs)

Two SMMs in the source set make the **same change**, effective **January 1, 2024**: an increase in the **Mandatory Cash-Out threshold to $7,000.**

### 5.1 AT&T Pension Benefit Plan SMM — July 2024 (NIN 78-72720)
Applies to **all twelve** programs of the Plan (including all six bargained programs in this reference, plus Legacy Management, Bargained Cash Balance, Mobility, Nonbargained, Southeast Management, and Southwest). It deletes and replaces four subsections in each program SPD:

1. **Time of Payment → Special Rule for Small Pension Benefits** — if present value ≤ **$7,000**, benefit is distributed or rolled over as soon as practicable after Termination of Employment (normal Benefit Commencement Date rules do not apply).
2. **Forms of Payment → Special Rule for Small Pension Benefits** — if present value ≤ **$7,000**, benefit is paid as a single lump sum (paid or rolled over).
3. **General Plan Information → Mandatory Cash-Out/Rollover Rules** — the ≤ $1,000 / $1,000–$7,000 tiers in §3.10 above.
4. **Funding of the Plan → Restrictions Based on Funding Level** — references the new **$7,000** lump-sum limitation threshold; restrictions apply only if the Plan's funding falls below federal thresholds (currently it does not).

### 5.2 AT&T Legacy Bargained Program SMM — July 2024 (NIN 78-72720)
A program-specific SMM for the **Legacy Bargained Program** containing the identical four changes. (Substantively duplicative of 5.1 as applied to that program.)

- **AT&T Retiree Benefit Changes — effective 1/1/2022** (announced Dec. 15–16, 2020): Four change types — (1) retiree medical when not yet Medicare-eligible, (2) **pension changes for the AT&T Legacy Management (ALM) and Nonbargained programs** (management programs, not the bargained programs in this reference), (3) retiree life insurance, and (4) retiree death benefit. Stated rationale: move retiree benefits "closer to market." Included for context; primarily affects management programs.
- **AT&T Southeast CWA Active Employee Guide (Oct. 2025)** — a plain-language active-employee benefits guide for the Southeast CWA population. Use as a member-facing companion to the Southeast Program SPD (§4.2).
- **Medical SPD 2021** — *held as a reference, not part of this pension consolidation* (per instruction). Consult it to clarify ambiguous health/medical-benefit language; it does not govern pension provisions.
